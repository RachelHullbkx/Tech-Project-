# The idea
Growing up playing hockey i notice how expensive equiptment was and it was super expensive for my sister becasue she was a goalie. All the time id outgrow perfectly good equipemnt and have to buy new stuff. Our target customers are parents who struggle with the cost of hockey gear or even parents just looking to save a little bit of money. Also athletes looking for affordable gear, and famillies who have unused gear sitting at home that they could sell. Sportswap is better than existing alternatives becasue it focuses specifically on sports equipment, It makes finding the right equipment easier, it saves families money, people can buy and sell locally without expensive shipping. Someone would choose SportSwap instead of lets say Facebook Marketplace because you can filter gear by sport, you are buying affordable local gear from famillies just like yours, you dont have to serach through unrelated stuff, and it gives perfectly good gear a second life. SportSwap will generate revenue by charging a 5% fee on every completed sale. Creating an account and listing equipment will be free to encourage more people to use the platform. For example, if someone sells a pair of hockey skates for $100, SportSwap will receive $5 and the seller will receive $95. 
# Where IT creates value
One area of the value chain where SportSwap uses information technology to create value is marketing and sales. The search bar helps customers save time by helping them find specific gear, also the filters allow customers to browse gear for their specific sport. And if your a seller you can advertise your gear to more potential buyers, customers can also browse gear from home any time they want.Another area of the value chain where SportSwap creates value is operations. First off, SportSwap is entirely online we don't need to rent a phyical store or warehouse. Sellers keep their equipment until someone buys it. This reduces operating costs and allows the business to reach more customers.
# Network effects
A two-sided network effect happens when a business connects two different groups of users, and each group benefits when the other group grows. This benefits SportSwaps buyers and sellers becasue More sellers give buyers a wider selection of equipment, sizes and prices, more buyers increase the chances of sellers selling their equipment quickly, as both groups grow, SportSwap becomes more valuable and attractive to new users.To overcome the cold start problem, SportSwap would partner with local youth sports associations.Including hockey, soccer, volleyball and basketball. These assoications would promote SportSwap throught emails, newsletters, and social media. Also encouraging parents to list gear their children have outgrown before the upcoming sports season.To demonstrate two sided nerwork effects, I included an interactive slider on the website called Stronger Together. Users can increase the simulated community size from 100 to 1000 members. The number of buyers, sellers and available gear listings increases. Buyers will have more equipment to choose from, and sellers can reach more potential customers. The simulation demonstrates how SportSwap becomes more valuable as its community grows.The simulation assumes 60% of members are buyers, 40% are sellers and each seller lists three items.
